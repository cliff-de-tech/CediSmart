Quick Overview & Core Architecture

CediSmart is engineered from first principles for the Ghanaian financial landscape—seamlessly connecting Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money), traditional bank accounts, and physical cash on hand with zero manual friction.

02

Onboarding, Authentication & Security

Ghanaian Phone Number Verification

  1. Launch CediSmart on your iOS or Android device (or open the CediSmart Web App).
  2. Enter your 9-digit or 10-digit Ghanaian mobile number (e.g., 024 123 4567). The country code +233 is automatically prefixed.
  3. Tap Continue to receive a 6-digit SMS verification code powered by Clerk.
  4. Enter the 6-digit OTP code to verify your phone ownership.

6-Digit Secure PIN & Biometrics

  • Hardware-Backed PIN: Create a personal 6-digit Security PIN stored inside your device's hardware Keychain (iOS) or Keystore (Android).
  • Face ID / Fingerprint Unlock: Enable biometric login during setup or anytime under Settings → Security → Biometric Login.
  • Auto-Lock & Privacy Shield: Configure automatic app lock (Immediate, 2 mins, 5 mins) and tap the eye icon on your Dashboard to mask all figures with GH₵ •••• in public.

05

Recording Transactions (Manual, SMS & Receipt Vision)

1. Manual Entry (+ Add Transaction)

Tap the center + button on the bottom navigation bar, choose Expense or Income, enter the amount in GH₵, pick your wallet and category, and tap Save Transaction.

2. Paste-to-Log MoMo SMS Rescue

Copy any raw transaction SMS from your messages (e.g., Payment received for GHS 150.00 from Kweku Mensah...) and either tap Import MoMo SMS Alert on the Add Transaction screen or paste it directly into AI Support (or the Web App's Gemini Parser). CediSmart extracts the amount, direction, merchant, and category automatically.

3. Multimodal Receipt & Invoice Vision Scanning

Open AI Support, tap the + attachment button, and snap a photo of any receipt (Shoprite, Melcom, Shell fuel station, pharmacy invoice). CediSmart's vision engine reads the merchant and total, presenting a 1-tap Confirm & Save to Ledger card.

06

MoMo & Bank SMS Auto-Sync

Eliminate manual data entry completely. Choose your device platform below to set up automatic transaction detection:

Android Direct Background SMS Interception

  1. Open CediSmart and go to Settings → Tracked Accounts & MoMo.
  2. Grant SMS Read Permissions when prompted by Android.
  3. Whenever an SMS arrives from MobileMoney, TelecelCash, ATMoney, or your bank, CediSmart detects it in the background and surfaces a 1-tap "Log Now" banner on your Home Dashboard.

Apple iOS Shortcuts Webhook Automation

Because iOS sandboxes SMS messages, CediSmart provides a secure cloud webhook that works with Apple's built-in Shortcuts app:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone → tap Automation → tap + (New Automation).
  2. Choose Message → set Sender to MobileMoney (or TelecelCash) → select Run Immediately.
  3. Choose New Blank Automation → add the Get Contents of URL action.
  4. Set the URL to the CediSmart SMS Webhook endpoint:
POST https://cedismart-api.onrender.com/api/v1/transactions/sms-webhook

Set Method to POST, add header Authorization: Bearer <Your_Token>, and set the JSON body:

{
  "sender": "MobileMoney",
  "message_body": "<Shortcut Input>",
  "phone": "+233XXXXXXXXX"
}
07

Smart Budgets, Safe-to-Spend & 50/30/20 Rule

Safe-to-Spend Today

Displayed live on both the Mobile Dashboard and Web App, Safe-to-Spend Today is calculated as:

Safe-to-Spend Today = (Total Monthly Budget − Total Spent This Month) ÷ Remaining Days in Month

50/30/20 AI Smart Budget Generator

Not sure how much to allocate per category? Enter your monthly take-home income in the Budgets tab and let CediSmart automatically distribute your limits across:

  • 50% Needs: Food & Dining, Utilities, Rent, Transportation, Healthcare.
  • 30% Wants: Shopping, Entertainment, Dining Out, Subscriptions.
  • 20% Savings & Debt: Emergency Fund, Investments, Loan Repayment.

Customizable Warning Thresholds

Under Settings → Notifications → Budget Warning Threshold, choose when CediSmart alerts you (50%, 75%, 80% default, 90%, or 100% of any category limit).

10

Multi-Profile Switching & Ghana Card KYC

Separate Personal & Business Ledgers

Keep your personal money and side-hustle or shop finances completely separate on one phone:

  1. Open Settings and tap Switch Profile inside the top Profile card.
  2. Tap Add Another Account to register or sign into a second phone number.
  3. Switch between profiles anytime using Face ID / Fingerprint or your 6-digit PIN. Each profile maintains its own isolated wallets, budgets, and PDF statements.

Ghana Card (Tier 1) Identity Verification

Open Settings → Profile → Identity Verification and enter your Ghana Card number (GHA-XXXXXXXXX-X) and Date of Birth to unlock Tier 1 verified status via NIA / Smile ID.
